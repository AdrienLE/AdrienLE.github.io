Birdie (My Inbox)

Birdie, also called My Inbox, is a personal email and task organizer developed by Adrien Ecoffet. It turns Gmail conversations and reminders into tasks, with summaries, grouping, read status, and snoozing.

With your permission, Birdie reads and searches Gmail, changes message labels, archives and restores conversations, and supports email composition and sending where enabled. It uses Google Drive files to synchronize task metadata, instructions, and organizer results across your devices.

An optional, separately authorized Codex organizer can assess email and other connected context, write task summaries, and carry out explicitly requested workflows. Birdie is a personal prototype for the developer and invited users; this page does not provide public registration.

Read the privacy policy for data access, storage, sharing, and removal, and the terms of use.
