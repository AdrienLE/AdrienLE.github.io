Privacy — Birdie (My Inbox)

Effective November 7, 2026

What Birdie accesses and why

Birdie uses Google authorization to access your account identity and Gmail messages, headers, labels, threads, and attachments. It uses this data to display and search mail, organize tasks, maintain read status, archive or restore mail, manage app snoozes, and compose or send email when those actions are enabled and requested. Its Gmail permission can permit more operations than a particular build exposes.

The Google Drive permission covers files created by Birdie or explicitly granted to it. These files synchronize task titles, summaries, grouping, message identifiers, read receipts, snooze deadlines, instructions, and organizer results. Task text may contain information derived from email; Birdie does not automatically copy complete mailbox bodies into these synchronization files.

Storage and service providers

Birdie caches mail and task data on your browser or device. Shared task data is stored in private files in your Google Drive account. The local macOS authorization broker stores refresh credentials in macOS Keychain; iOS sign-in uses Google's SDK and device Keychain. Access tokens are held in memory by the app.

If you enable the separately authorized Codex organizer, relevant Gmail content, task information, and other connected context can be processed by OpenAI's Codex service to interpret and organize your inbox. Its Google connections and service settings are separate from Birdie's client authorization. Google processes data for Gmail, Drive, and sign-in. Email you send or forward is shared with the recipients you choose.

Birdie does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Birdie's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Control, retention, and removal

You can disconnect the app and revoke its access through your Google Account connections. Revoke the organizer's separate connections and stop its automations if you also want its processing to stop. Disconnecting or revoking access does not automatically erase existing caches, Drive records, or Codex chats.

Local caches remain until you clear the app's account storage, browser site data, or device app data. You can delete Birdie's synchronization files from Drive and remove organizer chats and files using the relevant service controls. For help identifying app data or requesting removal of any copy held by the developer, contact the address below. Data retained by Google, OpenAI, or email recipients is also subject to those services' controls and policies.
